# Example Corp New Hire Onboarding Procedure

Document owner: People Operations. Version 1.8, effective 1 April 2026.

## Overview

Onboarding covers the period from signing the contract to the end of the probation period. The hiring manager owns the plan; People Operations, IT, and Facilities deliver their parts through the onboarding workflow in `hr.example.corp`.

## Before day one

- People Operations sends the welcome pack and collects the documents required for payroll and the right to work within **five business days** of the signed contract.
- IT creates the user account, mailbox, and Slack access **three business days** before the start date and ships the laptop **five business days** before the start date to remote hires.
- Facilities orders the access badge and, at the head office, a locker.
- The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the same team.

## Day one

- Badge collection at reception and a building tour by Facilities.
- Laptop handover and multi-factor authentication enrolment with the IT service desk. The temporary password must be changed at first sign-in, as required by the Password and Access Policy.
- Introduction meeting with the manager to review the role, the 30-60-90 day plan, and the team's ways of working.

## First week

- Complete the mandatory training modules within **five business days**: security awareness, data classification, and code of conduct. Access to client systems is granted only after security awareness training is complete.
- Meet the buddy daily for the first week, then weekly during the first month.
- Set up the expense account and read the Expense Reimbursement Policy and the Remote Work Policy.

## First 90 days

- 30-day check-in: manager and new hire review progress against the plan. People Operations sends a short survey.
- 60-day check-in: mid-probation review with written feedback.
- 90-day check-in: final probation assessment where the probation period is three months. Where local law sets a **six-month** probation period, a further review takes place at month five.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Contract and documents | People Operations | 5 business days after signing |
| Accounts and laptop | IT service desk | 3 to 5 business days before start |
| Badge and workspace | Facilities | Start date |
| Onboarding plan and buddy | Hiring manager | Start date |
| Mandatory training | New hire | First 5 business days |

## Leaving during probation

Either party may end the employment during probation with the notice period stated in the contract. Equipment is returned as described in the IT Equipment and Laptop Replacement Procedure.
