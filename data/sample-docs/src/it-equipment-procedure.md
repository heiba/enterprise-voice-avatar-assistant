# Example Corp IT Equipment and Laptop Replacement Procedure

Document owner: IT Service Management. Version 2.2, effective 15 March 2026.

## Standard equipment

| Profile | Standard device | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 14-inch laptop, 16 GB memory, 512 GB storage | Sales, People Operations, Finance, Project Management |
| Power | 16-inch laptop, 32 GB memory, 1 TB storage | Software Engineering, Data, Design, Video Production |

Every employee also receives a headset and may request one docking station and one external monitor of up to 27 inches for the office or home desk. Peripherals under **EUR 150** each (mouse, keyboard, webcam, cables) are provided on request without approval.

## Replacement cycle

- Laptops are replaced every **36 months**. The IT service desk contacts employees when their device reaches 34 months.
- Devices that fail before the end of the cycle are repaired under warranty where possible. If the repair takes longer than **five business days**, a loaner laptop is provided.
- Smartphones for on-call staff are replaced every 24 months.

## Requesting equipment

1. Open the IT service portal at `it.example.corp` and choose *Request equipment*, or describe the need to the assistant. The request is logged as a ticket with a reference such as REQ-000123.
2. The service desk classifies the request. Standard-profile devices for eligible roles are fulfilled without further approval. Power-profile devices, non-standard models, and any single item above **EUR 2,000** need approval from the department head.
3. Approved standard devices are delivered within **three business days** in Amsterdam, Dublin, and Berlin, and within ten business days to remote employees in other locations.
4. The employee confirms receipt in the portal. The asset is registered to the employee in the asset inventory.

## Damaged, lost, or stolen devices

- Report damage or loss to the IT service desk within **24 hours** at extension 4000 or `servicedesk@example.corp`. For a stolen device also file a police report and send the report number to the service desk.
- The service desk remotely locks and wipes lost or stolen devices.
- Accidental damage is covered by Example Corp. Repeated damage may be discussed with the manager.

## Returning equipment

- Equipment stays the property of Example Corp. On leaving the company, return all devices within **five business days** of the last working day. Data on returned devices is wiped before reuse.
- Employees changing profile return the old device when the new one is delivered.

## Software on devices

Devices are managed centrally. Employees have no local administrator rights; software is installed from the self-service catalogue or requested as described in the Software Request and Licensing Procedure.
