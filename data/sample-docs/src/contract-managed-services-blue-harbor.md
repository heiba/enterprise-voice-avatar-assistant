## 1. Services

Example Corp provides the following managed IT services to the Client:

- Service desk for the Client's 240 users, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 CET (8x5), by phone, email, and portal.
- Monitoring of the Client's servers, network, and cloud workloads 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24x7), with automated alerting.
- Patch management for servers and end-user devices on a monthly cycle, with emergency patches within 72 hours of a critical vulnerability notice.
- Backup of servers and file storage with daily incremental and weekly full backups, retained for 90 days, and a quarterly restore test.
- A named service delivery manager and a monthly service report.
