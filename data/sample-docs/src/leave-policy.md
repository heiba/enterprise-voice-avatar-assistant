# Example Corp Leave and Time Off Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Version 2.4, effective 1 January 2026. Local law takes precedence where it grants more than this policy.

## Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **25 days of annual leave** per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount.
- After every five full years of service one additional day is added, up to a maximum of **30 days**.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next year and must be taken by **31 March**. Days beyond that are forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.
- Requests are made in the HR portal at `hr.example.corp`. Requests of more than three consecutive days must be submitted at least **two weeks** in advance. Managers respond within five business days.
- Example Corp closes its offices between Christmas and New Year; those days are counted as annual leave unless the employee is scheduled to work.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the country of the employee's contract. The list is published in the HR portal each December.

## Sick leave

- Employees who are unwell notify their manager by **09:30 local time** on the first day of absence, by phone or Slack, and record the absence in the HR portal.
- A medical certificate is required from the **sixth consecutive calendar day** of absence, or earlier where local law requires it.
- Sick pay follows local law and the employment contract. Where local law allows, Example Corp pays 100 percent of salary during the first six months of illness.

## Parental and family leave

- Maternity, paternity, and parental leave follow the statutory schemes of each country. Example Corp tops up statutory maternity and paternity pay to full salary for the statutory period.
- Employees may take up to **two weeks of paid carer's leave** per year to care for a seriously ill family member.
- Bereavement leave of up to five paid days is available for the death of a close family member.

## Other leave

- Two paid days per year for moving house and one paid day for a wedding or civil partnership.
- Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted after two years of service with department head approval. Benefits continue during unpaid leave; salary does not.
- Time off for medical appointments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours is paid.

## Recording and questions

All absences are recorded in the HR portal. Questions go to People Operations at `people@example.corp`.
