## 1. Services

Blue Harbor provides the following managed IT services to Example Corp:

- Service desk for Example Corp's 240 users, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 local time (8x5), by phone, email, and portal.
- Monitoring of Example Corp's servers, network, and cloud workloads 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24x7), with automated alerting.

- A named service delivery manager and a monthly service report.
