# Example Corp Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document owner: Finance. Version 4.0, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all employees and contractors who incur business expenses on behalf of Example Corp.

## General rules

- Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business, and submitted in the expense system at `expenses.example.corp` within **30 days** of the date they were incurred. Claims older than 30 days need approval from the department head; claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.
- An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **EUR 25 or more**. For smaller amounts a receipt is recommended but not required.
- Use the corporate card where possible. Personal payments are reimbursed with the next payroll run after approval, at the latest within two pay cycles.
- Expenses are paid in euros. Foreign currency amounts are converted at the rate of the transaction date shown on the card statement or receipt.

## Approval thresholds

| Total claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 500 | Line manager |
| EUR 500 to EUR 5,000 | Department head |
| Above EUR 5,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Expenses of a manager are approved by the manager's own manager. Nobody may approve their own expenses.

## Travel

- Book flights, trains, and hotels through the travel desk (Meridian Travel) so that bookings are invoiced to Example Corp directly.
- Flights are booked in economy class. Business class is allowed for flights longer than **six hours** with department head approval.
- Train travel in second class is the default for trips under four hours. First class is allowed when it is cheaper than the economy flight alternative.
- Hotel rates are capped per night, excluding taxes: **head office city EUR 180, all other cities EUR 190**. Higher rates need department head approval before booking.
- Taxi and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport is not practical, for example late at night or when carrying equipment.
- Private car use is reimbursed at **EUR 0.23 per kilometre**, plus parking and tolls with receipts.

## Meals

- Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to **EUR 35 per day** for domestic travel and **EUR 60 per day** for international travel, including tips.
- Meals with clients or candidates are reimbursed at actual cost with the names of the attendees and the business purpose noted on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursed only for client meals.
- Lunch during a normal working day at the office is not reimbursed.

## Not reimbursed

Fines and penalties, personal entertainment, minibar items, upgrades without approval, travel insurance (Example Corp provides group cover), and expenses for family members travelling with an employee.

## Questions

Contact Accounts Payable at `ap@example.corp`. Policy exceptions are decided by the Chief Financial Officer.
