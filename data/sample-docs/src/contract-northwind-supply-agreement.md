# Master Supply Agreement

**Between:** Example Corp B.V., 200 Example Plaza, 1017 AB Amsterdam, the Netherlands ("Example Corp")
**And:** Northwind Office Supplies Ltd., 12 Harbour Road, Dublin D02 X285, Ireland, VAT IE9876543A ("Supplier")

**Agreement number:** MSA-2025-014
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Scope

The Supplier agrees to supply office equipment, IT peripherals, and consumables listed in the price list in Annex A to Example Corp's offices in Amsterdam, Dublin, and Berlin. Individual purchases are made through purchase orders referencing this agreement.

## 2. Term

This agreement runs for **two years** from the effective date and renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **90 days** before the end of the current period.

## 3. Ordering and delivery

- Purchase orders are placed through Example Corp's procurement system and are binding once acknowledged by the Supplier within two business days.
- Standard delivery is within **five business days** of acknowledgement to the office named on the purchase order. Delivery and installation charges are as listed in Annex A.
- Risk passes to Example Corp on delivery. Title passes on payment.

## 4. Prices and payment

- Prices are as stated in Annex A, in euros, excluding VAT. The Supplier may propose price changes once per year with 60 days' notice; changes apply only to orders placed after the new price list is accepted by Example Corp.
- Invoices are issued on delivery and are payable within **30 days** of the invoice date (net 30) by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice. Invoices must reference the purchase order number.
- Late payments accrue interest at **2 percent per month** on the outstanding amount.

## 5. Warranty

Hardware items are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for **24 months** from delivery. The Supplier repairs or replaces defective items within ten business days of notification, or refunds the purchase price if repair is not possible.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other party's non-public information confidential and uses it only to perform this agreement. This obligation survives termination for three years.

## 7. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. The total liability of each party under this agreement is limited to the amounts paid under this agreement in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim, except for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not remedy the breach within 30 days of notice, or becomes insolvent. Example Corp may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice.

## 9. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Disputes are submitted to the competent court in Amsterdam.
