# Example Corp IT Service Catalog

Document owner: IT Service Management. Version 5.0, effective 1 January 2026. All services are requested through the IT service portal at `it.example.corp`, by email to `servicedesk@example.corp`, by phone on extension 4000, or through the assistant.

## Service desk hours

Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time for Amsterdam and Berlin, 08:00 to 18:00 Irish time for Dublin. On-call support outside these hours applies to Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents only, as defined in the IT Incident Response Procedure.

## Priorities for requests

Requests are fulfilled in the order they are received. Requests for new hires with a start date within five business days are prioritised. Fulfilment targets are business days from approval, not from submission.

## Feedback

Every closed ticket sends a two-question survey. The IT Service Management team reviews scores monthly and publishes the results on the intranet.
